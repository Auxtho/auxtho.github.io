Page 4 of 6

RC10 - post-action uncertainty

The action may have started. The receipt is not trustworthy.

  1. Action started: One controlled attempt
  2. Receipt lost: Outcome cannot be proven
  3. State: Do not invent certainty
Control result: UNKNOWNRECONCILIATION REQUIRED / NO AUTOMATIC RETRY

Uncertainty is not failure. And it is not permission to try again.

The path preserves what is known without creating a second effect.
