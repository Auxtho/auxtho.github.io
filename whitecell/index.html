06 / EVIDENCE & HUMAN OVERSIGHTFrom records to human judgement

Preserve the evidence
Keep accountability clear

The architecture links WhiteCell observations and Ardamire enforcement evidence by case, placing the Live Operator Wall in Auxtho Console at the point of human oversight and intervention.

01 / RECORD

Add records Preserve originals

The principle is to append observations, analyses and corrections while preserving originals and earlier records. Separate verified facts from AI inference, including the reasons for a changed assessment.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Follow the whole case

Link material, versions and action history for forensic investigation. Keep original evidence, analysis and isolated replay results distinct, and trace the reasons and sequence behind each response.

03 / HUMAN OVERSIGHT

People oversee and intervene

Authorised operators use the Live Operator Wall to review cases, evidence, action status and unresolved questions. People verify and approve new defence policies and consequential changes or recovery, and retain final accountability.

↳Preauthorised, scoped protection and human oversight work together. Viewing the wall does not confer authority to enforce an action.
