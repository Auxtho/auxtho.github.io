What the work looks like

Write in your own voice. Return to what you kept. Ask when you want another perspective

WHY IT MATTERS

Keep the moment in your own words. Start with a line, without needing AI. Find it again in your archive, read the original, or begin a conversation about the entry you choose

HOW IT CONNECTS

Moirion is where you write, keep and revisit your journal. Our development builds also connect to a paired computer for a local AI response. Windows connections use Metdol; the paired-Mac implementation in Moirion applies Metdol's integration specification. Apple on-device AI runs separately on iPhone

Choose where the answer comes from

Use Apple on-device AI on a compatible iPhone, or choose a compatible conversation model installed in Ollama on your Mac. The conversation shows the AI path and model used

On Mac, write and draw in the same entry. In Glass, your marker can reach across the Moirion window, including menus and open panels. Choose your own colors, keep five favorites, and arrange them in the order you like. Pin a drawing as your background inside Moirion, then add new strokes over it

Keep documents alongside your notes

On Mac, import supported text, Markdown, PDF and Word (.docx) files into your archive

From your phone to your paired Mac model

What has been verified

Your journal on your phone. A local model on your computer

Development results show Moirion receiving local-model answers from Windows and Mac computers on the same local network, with the computer and model identified alongside the response

Open the record

20 SEPTEMBER 2026 / MAC → WINDOWSA local answer from another computer＋

Moirion's Mac development app sent a user-approved request through Metdol to a Windows RTX 5090 computer running Gemma 3 27B through Ollama. A Korean response returned to Moirion, with the computer and model shown in the app. The connection used pinned mutual TLS, and the one-shot listener closed after completion

RECORDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 / IPHONE → WINDOWSThe model runs on Windows. The answer comes back to iPhone＋

A real iPhone 18 Pro received a response from Gemma 3 27B through Windows Metdol. The direct-endpoint test authenticated the iPhone, verified the response and its model origin, and completed the synthetic “Return exactly BLUE.” request. The returned answer was BLUE, with a trailing newline normalized for comparison

5 OCTOBER 2026 / IPHONE → MACAn answer from your paired Mac＋

On the same local network, a real iPhone 18 Pro sent “Return exactly BLUE.” to its paired Mac running gemma4:e4b through Ollama. Moirion displayed BLUE and identified “Paired Mac · gemma4:e4b.” This question-only test included no journal entries or photos. The Mac connection is implemented in Moirion using Metdol's integration specification

8 OCTOBER 2026 / A SELECTED MAC ENTRY → IPHONE QUESTIONRevisit a Mac entry from your phone＋

In the development preview, an iPhone opened one explicitly selected Mac entry. With approval on both devices, the user asked “What did I do?” The paired Mac's gemma4:e4b model replied: The journal entry states, “I took a short walk after lunch.” The answer returned to the iPhone while the original entry remained on Mac; the iPhone view was temporary

These are completed development demonstrations on the recorded devices and builds. The Mac demonstrations use Moirion's implementation of Metdol's integration specification. The 9 October on-device iPhone gallery shows a separate installation and AI path

Before you begin

Can I write without AI?＋

Yes. Writing and browsing your journal work independently of AI

What do I need for AI on iPhone?＋

An Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone, with Apple Intelligence enabled and its on-device model ready. Moirion checks availability in the app

Can I choose my Mac model?＋

Yes. Choose a compatible local conversation model installed in Ollama on your Mac

Which languages are supported?＋

English is the primary language. Korean and French AI conversations are in alpha
