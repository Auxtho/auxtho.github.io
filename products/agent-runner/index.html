What the work looks like

A company-like structure for AI work. People define the objective and permitted scope. AI agents work by role, and returned work remains available for review

WHY IT MATTERS

Models evolve; projects still need continuity. Agent Runner develops and tests an operating structure that keeps goals, roles, work records and decisions available as AI teams work

HOW IT CONNECTS

Future direction: bring evaluated AI-team operations into Auxtho's internal Console when they are dependable enough for the intended repeated work. Feature completion alone will not determine adoption
