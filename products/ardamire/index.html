What the work looks like

From a refused request to authorised recovery, keep the affected target, the reason and the recorded outcome together

WHY IT MATTERS

A reviewed report needs controls through download and further processing. When a check fails, staff need to know what stopped, why it stopped and what is required to recover

HOW IT CONNECTS

Auxtho App is the customer workspace. Ardamire protects its connected processing paths, while Auxtho personnel investigate and manage recovery through the internal Console

See our products and their technology

01 / CHECK

Check who may do what

At connected processing boundaries, Ardamire checks identity, current authority and the permitted request state. Failed checks stop the affected request and retain the reason for investigation

02 / HOLD

Hold the affected target

Ardamire ties the issue to a specific file or operation. Targeted containment governs that target; unrelated work is checked separately. In the designated PDF test, the held PDF was blocked while the same job's JSON remained available

03 / RECOVER

Recover with authority

An authorised operator verifies the current file and hold before releasing its quarantine. Recovery preserves the file's approved content and records the reason, operator and time in the same incident. File recovery and incident closure are separate decisions
