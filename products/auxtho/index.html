What the work looks like

Start with one report sentence, not an architecture diagram. Auxtho prepares what needs checking, the person decides, and the approved work continues through a recorded output path

01 / PREPARE

Check the claim

Auxtho checks selected claims against approved sources and review rules, shows supporting passages and prepares exceptions with reasons

02 / DECIDE

Approve the exact version

The person corrects, approves, holds or rejects. A changed version cannot reuse approval for the earlier version

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Keep the result connected

Content and individual files have distinct decisions. Preserve the actual result—or an explicit unconfirmed state—for later reconstruction

CUSTOMER-FACING / AUXTHO APP

Review the work. Make the decision

The customer-facing App lets users inspect report findings and sources, make version-specific decisions, and review saved-file outcomes

INTERNAL / AUXTHO CONSOLE

Operate, investigate and re-examine

Auxtho personnel use Console for operations, evidence, incident handling and review. Replay links the original evidence, decision and result. Replay Lab compares an alternative policy version in a separate sandbox with the original record; it does not change that original record or automatically find the best decision
