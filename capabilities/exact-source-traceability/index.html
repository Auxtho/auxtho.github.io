Capability 01 / Exact Source Traceability

Find the exact wording behind a selected claim.

Auxtho opens the versioned source document, exact page, source-local wording, and matching passage on the original page. It keeps the source's role and release eligibility visible beside the evidence.

The reviewer sees the claim and its basis together instead of trusting a generated citation alone.

Inspect the product screenOpen the full Singapore proof

The job in plain language

A report sentence needs a source a reviewer can actually inspect.

The frozen example uses an AI-assisted financial assurance report and three public MAS documents with different review roles.

Document or job

A selected claim in a financial report

The reviewer needs to know whether the claim is supported by a current Notice, only clarified by an FAQ, or drawn from a proposal.

What Auxtho performs

Opens the exact page and passage

Auxtho binds the claim to exact source bytes, page 3, source-local wording, and the geometry of the matching passage.

What a person decides

Whether the claim should proceed

The accountable reviewer corrects, holds, or rejects a mismatch or proposed-only statement and makes the final decision.

What appears on screen

The sentence, source wording, and original page

The Evidence detail keeps the report sentence and the highlighted public source passage readable in one view.

What remains

An exact source trace tied to the review

The record retains source identity, version, page, passage, claim state, and the review context that used it.
