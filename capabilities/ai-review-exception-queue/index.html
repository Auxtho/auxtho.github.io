Capability 03 / AI Review & Exception Queue

Prepare the exceptions before the reviewer decides.

Auxtho records how claims entered review, compares each one with exact source proof, preserves the machine result and reason, and routes the items that need judgment to the accountable reviewer.

The reviewer receives the disputed claim, exact source correction, and reason together, then accepts, corrects, holds, or rejects it.

The job in plain language

A report contains claims that do not all need the same attention.

Auxtho performs the repeatable comparison and prepares the exception. The person decides how the disputed claim should change.

Review flow

Move repeatable comparison work to the system.

01Record claim selection

The run identifies whether each claim was user-specified, workflow-defined, rule-selected, or model-decomposed.

02Compare exact proof

The machine result retains source identity, page, wording, passage hash, and the reason behind its classification.

03Prepare the queue

Deterministic ordering sends supported work forward and presents exceptions with their reasons and owner role.

04Record the human disposition

The reviewer accepts, corrects, holds, or rejects the prepared result; the original finding remains preserved.

05Recheck before authorization

Changed artifact, source pack, rule, claim set, route, target, or authority invalidates the previous review binding.
