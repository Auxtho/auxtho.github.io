Capability 06 / Ardamire Defense Layer

Protect the work Keep recovery accountable

Ardamire is Auxtho's implemented internal defence layer. It checks identity, authority and request state in connected processing paths, links problems to specific files or operations, and keeps investigation and authorised recovery with the same case.

Operators can see what stopped, why it stopped and who authorised recovery, without losing the original record.

The job in plain language

Investigate the exception Restore permitted work

Identity and request checks, targeted holds, evidence and recovery history stay connected. Consequential decisions remain with authorised people.

Document or job

An affected file or request

A report download is checked against the requester's authority, the current request state and the recorded file.

What Ardamire performs

Checks, targeted holds and case records

Failed checks stop the affected request. Related signals, hold reasons and execution evidence support investigation in the internal Console.

What a person decides

Whether recovery is authorised

An authorised operator checks the current target and hold before releasing quarantine. File recovery and incident closure are separate decisions.

What appears on screen

Investigation and re-examination

Watch gathers case-scoped evidence candidates and handoff records for operator review. Ardamire Agent explains supported Console screens and the next checks. Replay Entry leads to review and separate Lab re-execution.

What remains visible

The original record and recovery history

Target, reason, operator and time remain linked to the incident. Separate replay results can be compared without replacing the original record or approval.

Public modeled signal scenario

Investigate first Decide explicitly Verify before rollout

This six-stage illustration explains the path from a security signal to investigation, a proposed change, human review and verification. The linked animation walks through this model. Dated implementation and test records are available on the Ardamire product page.

01Detect

A security signal enters the defensive control path.

02Quarantine

The affected path enters a hold while the signal is investigated.

03Analyze

Signal, artifact, and policy context are assembled for review.

04Harden proposal

A defensive change is prepared as a proposal, not executed.

05Human review

An accountable person decides whether the proposal advances.

06Verify

The approved proposal enters verification before rollout.
