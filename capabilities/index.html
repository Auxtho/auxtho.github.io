Five-minute product chain

See the complete basis, decision, action, and result chain.

01Approved source and AI-assisted work

Start with the exact source set and the response, report, or analysis prepared for review.

02Selected claims and prepared exceptions

Auxtho finds the exact wording, compares the selected claims, and routes the disputed items with reasons.

03Human correction and final decision

The accountable reviewer inspects the evidence, resolves the exception, and approves, holds, or rejects the exact review.

04Exact-version authorization and permitted action

The decision binds to one exact reviewed version and one permitted next action; changed work cannot reuse it.

05Receipt, UNKNOWN, or reconciliation

The result is recorded only after verified receipt readback confirms it. An uncertain result stays UNKNOWN and does not trigger automatic retry.

06Durable history, reconstruction, and recovery

Authorized reviewers reopen the exact receipt and reconstruct the source, rule, person, action, and result when challenged.
