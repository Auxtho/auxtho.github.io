Capability 05 / Incident Reconstruction & Recovery

Reconstruct what happened. Recover only what is known.

Auxtho reconstructs which document, source set, rules, person, decision, authorization, action, and receipt were involved. It scopes the affected release, prepares a response record, and verifies recovery readback before accepting a result.

When a result cannot be verified, Auxtho records UNKNOWN and stops instead of repeating a possibly consequential action.

Follow the reconstructionSee the UNKNOWN case

The job in plain language

A reviewed document changed, or its downstream result became unclear.

The response team needs the exact chain that produced the event before it decides what to notify, correct, retry, or close.

Document or job

A reviewed financial report and its release record

The synthetic case starts with a changed-artifact signal tied to an exact review and controlled release chain.

What Auxtho performs

Reconstructs and scopes the event

Auxtho binds the artifact, sources, policies, human decision, authorization, consequence, and receipt, then derives the exact affected-release inventory.

What a person decides

Notification, remediation, and closure

An accountable person decides what the organization should notify or remediate and whether the incident can close.

What appears on screen

Impact, decision boundary, and response package

The App keeps the exact affected scope, unresolved rule status, and the verified response package visible as separate records.

What remains

A reconstruction, receipt, or UNKNOWN state

The historical record remains unchanged while recovery stores its readback evidence and final disposition.

One incident path

From signal to exact affected scope and human closure.

01Detect the change

A content-free signal identifies that the reviewed artifact commitment changed.

02Rebuild the exact chain

Auxtho reconstructs the reviewed artifact, selected claims, source pack, policies, authority, action, and receipt.

03Scope affected releases

Exact lineage identifies the affected release. Filenames, titles, and mutable “latest” pointers are not used as substitutes.

04Separate facts and decisions

Observed facts, system inferences, unresolved questions, human decisions, and remediation commitments remain distinct.

05Close with a bound package

The response package records the exact incident state, impact inventory, decisions, and remediation evidence.
