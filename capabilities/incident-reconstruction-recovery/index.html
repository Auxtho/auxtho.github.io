Capability 05 / Incident Reconstruction & Recovery

Reconstruct what happened. Recover only what is known.

Auxtho reconstructs the report version, sources, rules, reviewer, decision, permitted action, and recorded result. It identifies affected releases, prepares the response record, and verifies receipt readback before treating the result as known.

When a result cannot be verified, Auxtho records UNKNOWN and stops instead of repeating a possibly consequential action.

Follow the reconstructionSee the UNKNOWN case

The job in plain language

A reviewed document changed, or its downstream result became unclear.

The response team needs the exact chain that produced the event before it decides what to notify, correct, retry, or close.

Document or job

A reviewed financial report and its release record

The case starts with a recorded signal that a reviewed report changed and links that signal to the decision and release records.

What Auxtho performs

Reconstructs and scopes the event

Auxtho reconstructs the report, sources, rules, human decision, permitted action, and result, then identifies exactly which releases were affected.

What a person decides

Notification, remediation, and closure

When the authoritative notification rule is unavailable, the system escalates the gap and an accountable person determines the applicable response and whether the incident can close.

What appears on screen

Affected releases, the unresolved rule, and the response record

The screen keeps the affected releases, unresolved notification rule, and reviewed response record visible as separate records.

What remains

A reconstruction, confirmed result, or unresolved state

The historical record stays unchanged while recovery stores the confirmation checked and the final result status.

One incident path

From signal to exact affected scope and human closure.

01Detect the change

A content-free signal identifies that the exact reviewed report version changed.

02Rebuild the exact chain

Auxtho reconstructs the reviewed artifact, selected claims, source pack, policies, authority, action, and receipt.

03Scope affected releases

Stored links identify the affected release. Filenames, titles, and “latest” labels are not treated as proof.

04Separate facts and decisions

Observed facts, system inferences, unresolved questions, human decisions, and remediation commitments remain distinct.

05Close with a bound package

The response package records the exact incident state, impact inventory, decisions, and remediation evidence.

Recovery example

A receipt readback decides whether the outcome is known.

The recovery plan pins the exact release chain and receiver identity. Auxtho accepts the result only when authenticated readback from that pinned receiver verifies it.

Signed receipt found

Auxtho verifies the pinned receiver and exact signed receipt, then records the confirmed result.

CONFIRMED SUCCESS
Exact receipt replay

The same verified receipt is returned without increasing the effect or dispatch count.

NO NEW EFFECT
Receipt missing or mismatched

The system does not dispatch again. It records the unresolved outcome for a person to reconcile.

UNKNOWN / NO AUTOMATIC RETRY
