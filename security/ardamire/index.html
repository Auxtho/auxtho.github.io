Bounded investigation

Ardamire keeps investigation context separate from decision authority.

Ardamire names the Auxtho-scoped surfaces used to inspect a selected context, compare available evidence, preserve a reviewable handoff, and leave the final disposition with a person.

Bounded investigation and evidence surfaces within the Auxtho workflow. This page does not establish live enforcement, incident prevention, or operating effectiveness.

Visible product boundary

Investigate a selected case without turning evidence into an instruction.

The surface is designed around explicit context and human follow-up. An identifier can locate reviewed context; it does not grant access or confer authority.

01 Select context

Investigation begins from an explicitly selected Auxtho case.

02 Inspect evidence

Available lineage, replay, and signature context can be reviewed.

03 Record attention

Case-bound signals can be surfaced for human follow-up.

04 Prepare handoff

Review-relevant evidence stays linked to the selected context.

05 Human disposition

A person decides what remains held and what may move forward.

Current public claim

A product surface, not a customer-wide defense claim.

Exact-context investigation

Investigation remains scoped to the selected Auxtho context and its available evidence. This page does not claim unrestricted discovery across customer systems.

Replay and signature posture

Reviewers can use replay- and signature-oriented context where present. A visible issue remains evidence for review, not an executable instruction.

Case-bound Watch

Attention can remain attached to the selected case. The public claim does not extend to autonomous monitoring, automatic remediation, or live incident response.

Evidence-linked handoff

Review context can be prepared for a human handoff while release and export stay separately bounded. Ardamire does not acquire approval authority.

Authority and assurance limits

What this page deliberately does not claim.

  • Ardamire does not replace a customer's SOC, SIEM, EDR, IAM, incident-response process, or security controls.
  • No live customer case, production incident prevention, detection rate, response time, or operating effectiveness is asserted here.
  • No compliance approval, regulatory suitability, certification, or independent assurance is implied.
  • No provider runtime, customer data, credentials, policy rules, detection thresholds, or exploit details are disclosed.
  • Review, approval, release, and export decisions remain human and separately controlled.

Relationship to the product

A side surface for exceptions, not the main workflow.

Auxtho's primary public story remains selected AI-assisted work, available evidence, human review, and a bounded release outcome. Ardamire supports investigation when a selected context needs closer human attention; it does not replace that path or broaden the public product claim.
