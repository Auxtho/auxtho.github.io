Ardamire Defense Layer

Ardamire maps six control stages from bounded signal detection to verification before rollout. It keeps the signal, investigation context, defensive proposal, and accountable human decision in one visible sequence.

Control structure

Separate the signal, proposal, decision, and verification.

Ardamire keeps each consequential transition visible. A signal can be investigated and a defensive proposal can be prepared without silently inheriting authority to continue or roll out.

Inspect the signal

Bring a bounded signal and its available context into one inspectable path.

Bound the proposal

Keep a defensive change separate from execution while its supporting context is examined.

Record the decision

Continue to verification only after an accountable person explicitly approves the next stage.

Enterprise fit

Map Ardamire into the controls already in use.

  • A scoped evaluation can map Ardamire against SOC, SIEM, EDR, IAM, incident-response, and security-control environments.
  • Integrations, responsibilities, and evidence requirements are agreed for each operating environment.
  • Review, approval, release, and export decisions remain with designated people and controlled systems.
