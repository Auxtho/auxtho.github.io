Research lineage

ISP records the research lineage behind a bounded AI workflow.

The ISP prototype made one operating idea inspectable: an AI-assisted request can move through a bounded run, visible state, human review, and a closing decision without giving the AI final authority.

Public research lineage and prototype workflow proof. ISP is not presented here as the current Auxtho production core or as evidence of production operating effectiveness.

Public workflow proof

A visible path with an explicit stopping point.

The public proof focuses on the sequence a reviewer can inspect. It does not publish the internal enforcement logic behind that sequence.

01 Request

A bounded task is selected for the prototype run.

02 Bounded run

The run stays inside the declared prototype scope.

03 Visible state

Review-relevant state is surfaced rather than implied.

04 Human review

A person evaluates the visible result and context.

05 Closing decision

The run ends with an explicit reviewed disposition.

Relationship to Auxtho

Lineage, not a claim that the prototype is the product.

What carries forward

Bounded work, visible state, evidence-linked review, explicit authority, and a recorded closing decision remain part of the design lineage.

What remains separate

The ISP proof package is a research artifact. It does not establish the deployment, scale, customer adoption, integrations, or operating effectiveness of the current Auxtho product.

Evidence boundary

What the public reference does and does not establish.

Established by the public package

A separately published prototype flow, its reviewed artifacts, and the relationship between visible workflow states and human review.

Not established

Production readiness, regulatory suitability, customer outcomes, live telemetry, external-system integration, or a current product deployment.

  • Internal policy logic, fallback chains, credentials, provider configuration, and enforcement details are not published.
  • The proof package is evidence of a prototype workflow, not third-party certification or independent assurance.
  • The current Auxtho product must be evaluated through its own reviewed product evidence and pilot scope.
