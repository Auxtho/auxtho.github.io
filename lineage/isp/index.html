Protocol lineage

Intent verification inside the earlier Auxtho Core architecture.

Historically, Auxtho Core described the broader execution-control architecture. ISP was the intent-verification protocol within it: check whether a generated plan remained aligned with the requested outcome before execution was permitted.

That principle continues in Auxtho App and Auxtho Console: intent, evidence, authority, and outcome remain visible at the handoff where an accountable person decides what moves forward.
