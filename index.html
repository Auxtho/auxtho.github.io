AUXTHO APP + AUXTHO CONSOLE

Approval-gated, evidence-linked release control for AI-assisted work.

Auxtho gives risk, compliance, and operations teams a controlled release path for selected AI-assisted work. It links each output to its evidence, accountable reviewer, decision state, and recorded human outcome before release. Auxtho App assembles the review workspace. Auxtho Console surfaces exceptions and follow-up.

See the Review Path Map One Workflow

THE REVIEW GAP

When AI-assisted work moves, its evidence and decision should move with it.

Output, evidence, accountability, and the release decision often separate across tools. Auxtho keeps them in one visible control path so the decision does not have to be reconstructed later.

Auxtho controls the work-level handoff: whether a selected AI-assisted output moves forward with its evidence and accountable human decision attached.
