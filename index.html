FOR REGULATED FINANCIAL TEAMS

AI can do the work. A person must own the consequence.

Auxtho reads the financial source documents used to prepare AI-assisted complaint responses and compliance or assurance reports. It finds the relevant pages and exact wording, checks selected claims against approved sources and review rules, and prepares exceptions for the accountable reviewer. The final decision stays tied to the exact reviewed version and recorded result.

HOW IT WORKS

From financial documents to the exact basis behind the final decision.

Auxtho first reads the documents and finds the relevant source pages. Release Core then carries those findings into the exact reviewed version, permitted next action, and recorded result.

  1. 01Read and organize the sources

    Process PDF evidence page by page while preserving text, tables, and page identity.

  2. 02Find the relevant pages and wording

    Find and rank the passages that answer the question, then open the original page for inspection.

  3. 03Check claims and prepare exceptions

    Compare selected claims with approved sources and review rules; route gaps and conflicts.

  4. 04Decide, release, and record

    The accountable reviewer decides; Auxtho keeps the exact version, permitted action, and result together.

RELEASE CORE

Keep the decision attached to the exact thing that moves forward

Release Core binds the reviewed work to its sources, rules, human authority, permitted next action, and recorded result.

  • Previous authorization stops applying when the reviewed artifact changes
  • Result uncertainty becomes UNKNOWN and reconciliation, not invented success
  • No automatic retry when the consequence cannot be confirmed
