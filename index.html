ARDAMIREIndependent enforcement

Ardamire WhiteCell

A real-time AI defence architecture for observing anomalies in incoming requests and internal workflows. Suspicious activity is contained, reproduced and analysed in isolation to prepare a defensive response.

WhiteCell records its investigation and proposes a response. Ardamire independently governs actual actions under its own authority and policies.
