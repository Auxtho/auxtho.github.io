FOR REGULATED TEAMS RESPONSIBLE FOR CUSTOMER RESPONSES AND CLIENT REPORTS

Auxtho lets the approved version move forward and stops the changed one.

For AI-assisted customer responses and client reports, a person makes the final decision. Auxtho enforces it at the sending step and prevents an automatic resend when the result is unconfirmed.

WHY NOW

Approval should not follow a changed version.

A person approves one version of a customer response or client report. If another version reaches the sending step, that approval no longer applies. Auxtho stops it and returns it for review.

01Draft preparedAI helps prepare a response, report, or other document.
02Version approvedA person reviews the content and decides whether it can move forward.
03Approval enforcedBefore sending, Auxtho enforces the decision against the version that earned it.

A matching version moves forward. A mismatch stops. If the result is unconfirmed, Auxtho does not send the work again automatically.

HOW IT WORKS

Keep approval with the version that earned it.

Auxtho records the reviewed version, the person who approved it, and the result at the sending step.

  1. 01Name the document

    Choose the customer response, client report, or other work being controlled.

  2. 02Review the version

    Link the reviewed document to the evidence considered and the responsible reviewer.

  3. 03Make the decision

    A designated person approves, holds, or rejects that version.

  4. 04Enforce and record

    At the sending step, Auxtho lets the approved version move forward, stops a mismatch, and records the result.
