WHY NOW

AI moves in seconds. Accountability cannot arrive days later.

When outputs, evidence, ownership, and decisions separate across tools, the most expensive question arrives after the work has already moved: why was this released?

01Work movesAn AI-assisted output is ready quickly.
02Context fragmentsEvidence, ownership, and rationale live elsewhere.
03Review becomes reconstructionTeams rebuild the decision after the fact.

If the reviewed object changes, its release authority no longer applies. If delivery becomes uncertain, Auxtho moves the record to reconciliation instead of inventing success or retrying blindly.

HOW IT WORKS

One controlled path from work to release.

For the selected workflow, a human decision is a required, recorded step before work is marked release-ready or enters controlled export.

  1. 01Select the workflow

    Choose the AI-assisted work and the consequential action that follows it.

  2. 02Link the context

    Bring the output, supporting evidence, and accountable owner into review.

  3. 03Make the decision

    A designated person approves, holds, or rejects the work.

  4. 04Preserve the record

    Keep the outcome and the evidence behind it available for later review.
